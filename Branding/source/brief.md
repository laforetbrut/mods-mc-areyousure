# Brief: Are You Sure?

## The project

A Minecraft troll mod for NeoForge and Forge (1.21.1 and 26.1.2). Before the player breaks, hits,
places or opens anything, a red dialog asks "ARE YOU SURE?", then a captcha must be typed back.
One captcha unlocks one kind of action. Server admins can target players with `/areyousure`.

Audience: players who prank their friends, YouTubers doing challenge runs, server admins running
events. They find it on CurseForge and Modrinth, among thousands of mods, at 96 px in a list.

## Personality

Troll, loud, funny (chosen by the user). Not mean, not horror: the joke is an absurd,
over-zealous safety check.

## What the logo must make someone feel

"Wait... really?" The hesitation before an action, in one glance. It must read as a question
before anything else, and as Minecraft second.

## Constraints

- Name as written: "Are You Sure?". Tagline: "Are you really sure?".
- Holds at 16 px (mod list, favicon) and in one color.
- Colors: the red of the in-game dialog header (`#e0453a`) and its dark panel (`#141419`) are
  already the mod's look; the CurseForge page theme uses them.
- The existing "official" logo is a red MS Paint scrawl. The new mark replaces it; the scrawl can
  stay as a joke on the page.
- Never the Minecraft logo, Mojang art or a Mojang font.

## Avoid

- A troll face, TNT, a rickroll: what every troll mod uses.
- reCAPTCHA's circular arrows and the ticked checkbox: someone else's brand.
- The yellow warning triangle: Windows, and every "alert" icon.
- A question mark in a circle: the generic "help" icon.

## Ideas, in words

1. A question mark whose dot is an empty checkbox: the question and the captcha in one sign.
2. A pickaxe frozen mid-swing, its head bent into the hook of a question mark.
3. The mod's own dialog window: a red title bar, a question mark inside.
4. A pig (the first victim on the page) with question marks for eyes.
5. "??" two marks, the second one bigger: insistence.
6. A cursor hesitating between two buttons.
7. A block with a question mark carved in it, not yet broken.
8. A question mark made of captcha letters, each a different color.
9. An "AYS?" monogram.
10. A speech bubble shaped like a dialog box.
11. A hand pulled back from a block.
12. A question mark whose stem is a held key (the attack button) that cannot go down.

Combinations kept: 1 (question plus captcha), 2 (question plus every action), 3 (the mod's own UI),
4 (the funny one).

## Landscape

Researched on 2026-10-16:

- Troll and prank mods on Modrinth (https://modrinth.com/mod/trolling-mod,
  https://modrinth.com/mod/redstonos-troll-mod, https://modrinth.com/mod/troll-gui,
  https://modrinth.com/mod/the-bad-joke, https://modrinth.com/project/v1WUOCjr): screenshots,
  memes, troll faces and TNT as icons. None has a designed mark; at 96 px they read as noise.
- Captcha brands (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RecaptchaLogo.svg,
  https://www.vectorlogo.zone/logos/google_recaptcha/index.html): circular arrows in blue,
  the ticked checkbox. Owned by others: not used.
- Confirmation dialogs (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/uxguide/mess-confirm):
  the warning triangle and the "?" in a circle are the system clichés.
- Lookalike search for "Are You Sure" logos found no product mark with that name.

Clichés to avoid: meme faces, explosives, circular arrows, ticked checkbox, triangle, "?" in a
circle.

Opening: no troll mod has a clean, loud, single-sign mark. A blocky question mark in the dialog's
red, built on the game's pixel grid, with one twist that says "captcha" or "every action", stands
out in a list of screenshots and still reads at 16 px.
