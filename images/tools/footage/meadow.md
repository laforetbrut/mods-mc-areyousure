# Scene: meadow

The hero banner of the CurseForge page: the pig and the oak of the capture scenes, seen while the
camera turns slowly around them. Nothing happens; the camera move is the motion.

- World: `aysure_flat` (superflat, made by images/tools/capture/scenes.txt, which builds the oak,
  the flowers and the pig at 1.0 -60 3.0).
- Stage: spectator, invisible, morning light, daylight and weather stopped, dropped items removed,
  the recording player high above the set (out of every shot).
- Length: 20 seconds.
